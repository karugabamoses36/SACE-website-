About SACE

Our Mission

Empower Ugandan communities through education, human rights, health access and poverty alleviation.

Our Vision

A world free from health and socio-economic injustices where communities thrive sustainably.

Philosophy

Youth & women are the core drivers of socio-economic development and lasting change.

History

Founded in 2017, SACE has become a leading force for community transformation across West Nile, Uganda.

What We Do

Education Support

Scholarships, school supplies, and after-school mentorship for vulnerable children.

Health Awareness

Medical outreaches, WASH programs, and reproductive health education in remote areas.

Youth Empowerment

Digital literacy, entrepreneurship bootcamps, and leadership training for economic independence.

Flagship Projects

School Infrastructure

Built 4 classrooms and libraries, boosting enrollment by 45% in target areas.

Eco-Warriors

Community cleanups & recycling drives — planting 5,000+ trees across Arua.

Skills for Livelihoods

Tailoring, shoe making & agribusiness training, 350+ women now running small enterprises.
