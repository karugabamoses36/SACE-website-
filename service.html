 Service Highlight

📚 Education Support

We believe education is the cornerstone of lasting change. SACE provides holistic support to children from vulnerable backgrounds, ensuring they stay in school and excel.

  • Scholarships — Full and partial tuition for primary & secondary students.
  • School supplies — Books, uniforms, and learning materials delivered termly.
  • Mentorship — After-school tutoring and career guidance sessions.
  • Parent engagement — Workshops to build supportive home learning environments.

 Service Highlight

💊 Health & WASH

Access to healthcare and clean water is a basic right. Our health programs target the most underserved communities in the West Nile region.

  • Medical outreaches — Free consultations, malaria testing, and deworming.
  • WASH programs — Borehole drilling, sanitation training, and hygiene promotion.
  • Reproductive health — Youth-friendly services and maternal health education.
  • Community health workers — Training locals to deliver primary care.

 Service Highlight

💻 Youth Empowerment

Young people are the engine of change. We equip them with future-ready skills, entrepreneurship mindsets, and leadership capabilities.

  • Digital literacy — Computer skills, coding, and online safety.
  • Entrepreneurship bootcamps — Business planning, finance, and marketing.
  • Leadership training — Civic engagement, public speaking, and community organizing.
  • Internship linkages — Connecting graduates to local employers.

 Service Highlight

🏢 School Infrastructure

A conducive learning environment is essential for quality education. We build and renovate schools to create safe, inspiring spaces for children.

  • Classroom construction — 4 new classrooms completed, boosting enrollment by 45%.
  • Libraries — Stocked with books and reading materials for all ages.
  • Sanitary facilities — Gender-separated latrines with handwashing stations.
  • Playgrounds — Safe spaces for recreation and sports.

 Service Highlight

🌍 Eco-Warriors

Environmental sustainability is at the heart of our work. We mobilize communities to take action against climate change and protect natural resources.

  • Tree planting — 5,000+ trees planted across Arua and surrounding districts.
  • Cleanup campaigns — Monthly community cleanups and recycling drives.
  • Environmental education — Workshops on waste management and conservation.
  • Climate advocacy — Youth-led campaigns for policy change.

 Service Highlight

🧵 Skills for Livelihoods

Economic empowerment starts with practical skills. We provide hands-on vocational training that enables women and youth to build sustainable livelihoods.

  • Tailoring & fashion — Sewing, design, and small-business management.
  • Shoemaking — Crafting durable footwear for local markets.
  • Agribusiness — Sustainable farming, poultry, and value addition.
  • Business mentorship — 350+ women now running their own enterprises.
