Tukashaba Felix

Executive Director & Founder, SACE

Tukashaba Felix is the heart and driving force behind Stride Action Community Empowerment (SACE). With an unwavering commitment to social justice and educational equity, Felix founded SACE to bridge critical gaps in underserved regions. His visionary leadership has transformed thousands of lives through strategic mentorship, scholarship pipelines, and community-led resilience projects.

Under his direct guidance, SACE has become a beacon of hope, launching award-winning youth leadership forums, digital literacy campaigns, and economic empowerment initiatives. Felix’s holistic approach blends modern advocacy with grassroots solidarity, making him a respected voice in regional development.
