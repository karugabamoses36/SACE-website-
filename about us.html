About SACE

We are a community-driven organization committed to empowering vulnerable communities in Uganda through education, health, and sustainable livelihoods.

Our Compass

Guided by a clear purpose, we work every day to build a future where every community thrives.

Our Mission

Empower Ugandan communities through education, human rights advocacy, health access, and poverty alleviation — creating lasting, self-sustaining change.

Our Vision

A world free from health and socio-economic injustices, where communities thrive sustainably and every individual reaches their full potential.

Our Philosophy

We believe youth and women are the core drivers of socio-economic development. Empowering them creates a ripple effect that transforms entire communities.

Our Story

From a small initiative in 2017 to a leading force for community transformation across West Nile, Uganda.

How It All Began

Stride Action Community Empowerment (SACE) was founded in 2017 by a group of passionate Ugandans who saw the urgent need for holistic community development in the West Nile region. What started as a small youth-led movement has grown into a trusted organization reaching thousands of lives.

Over the years, we have built classrooms, drilled boreholes, trained entrepreneurs, and provided health outreach to remote villages. Our work is driven by the belief that every person — regardless of background — deserves a chance to thrive.

  • 50+ communities reached
  • 500+ children supported
  • 150+ youth empowered
